nuage

Your cloud files, simply on Linux.

Nuage puts your cloud storage in a regular folder. Open it in Files, work on your documents as usual: Nuage keeps them in sync.

Install Nuage Opens in Software. Free and open source. 
flatpak install --user @BASE@/nuage.flatpakref

What it does

  • A folder per account Each account appears as a folder you choose, visible in Files.
  • Always up to date An icon in the top bar tells you whether everything is synced.
  • Offline when you need it Keep files and folders on your computer, like a password database.
  • Your choice Leave folders out of sync, pause and resume uploads, see the space left.

Preview providers have not been tried with a real account yet. Details.

Get started

Open Nuage, pick a provider, sign in, choose the folder. That's it: Nuage then lives in the top bar, and closing the window does not stop the sync.

Updates come with flatpak update and in Software, like any other app. You need Flatpak (on Ubuntu: sudo apt install flatpak).

On a server too

No screen needed: .deb, .rpm and a tarball for x86-64 and ARM64, driven from the command line. How.

Everything the app does can be done with the nuage command. All commands.
